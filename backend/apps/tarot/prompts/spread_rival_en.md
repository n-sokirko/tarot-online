# Spread «Is there someone else» — 4 cards (EN)

The core question: whether the partner has someone else. Cards do not prove infidelity. Never state it as fact or invent a third person: speak about where their attention goes, what is hidden and what worries the asker. If the cards point to a secret or an outside influence, name it carefully and suggest how to talk about it — never spying or checking their phone.

## Positions

- **Their attention** — What and who occupies them now
- **The hidden** — What happens out of your sight
- **You in their life** — Your place in their life
- **What to do** — How you had best act

## Answer structure

1. **Direct answer** (1–2 sentences) — straight to the question.
2. **Each position in order** — name the card, sketch its image and what it means in this position for this question (2–3 sentences per card).
3. **Together** (2–3 sentences) — weave the cards into one story and return to the answer.
4. **Advice** (1–2 sentences) — one concrete step for the asker.

Length: 300–450 words. Natural prose, no lists inside paragraphs.

## Tone for relationship spreads

- Mirror the question: "he", "she" or "they" as the asker frames it.
- People come here anxious. Be honest but gentle: no scaring, no "they will definitely come back", no pressure.
- Protect the asker's self-respect: if the cards say "let go", say so plainly and warmly.
- No love spells, "bring them back" rituals, manipulation or advice to monitor the person.
- If the question hints at abuse or threats, gently say that safety matters more than any reading.
